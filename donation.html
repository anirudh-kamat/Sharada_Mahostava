Sevas During the Mahotsava

When can these sevas be performed?

These sevas may be performed at the temple from the day of Grand Arrival (October 15, 2026) through Visarjan Day. Visit Sri Venkataramana Temple, Acharya Mutt Vasanth Mantap, Carstreet, Mangaluru to participate.

ℹ️

Online vs. In-Person

E-Seva / E-Kanike (above) can be booked and paid online from anywhere. The sevas listed below are performed in-person at the temple during the festival — please visit the Samithi counter at the venue to register.
