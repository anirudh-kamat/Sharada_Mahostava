Historical Journey

✦

Since the early 1920s

Shree Sharada Mahotsava at Acharya Mutt Vasantha Mantap, Carstreet is a century-old public celebration of Goddess Sharada (Saraswati) held at Sri Venkataramana Temple, Mangaluru. The festival is a sacred offering of devotion, culture, and community — drawing thousands of faithful devotees every year to seek Her blessings.

  • Begun in the early 1920s by the GSB community; now a festival for all.
  • Observed during Sharan Navaratri over 5–10 days with daily poojas and alankaras.
  • Centenary celebrated in 2022; continues with greater grandeur and inclusivity.
Key Rituals
Vidyarambha (learning initiation), Rangapuja, Vishesha Deepalankara and Grand Dasara Procession (Shobha Yatra).
